What we do

A property business, plainly put

Our business is the buying and selling of real estate that the company owns itself.

Buying property

We acquire property on the company’s own account, as the buyer in the transaction.

Selling property

We sell property from the company’s own holdings, as the seller in the transaction.

Hertfordshire based

The company is based in Waltham Cross, in the Borough of Broxbourne on the Hertfordshire–London border.
